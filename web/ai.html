      What the engine is and is not

      The engine estimates, at a clinic visit 1–24 months after kidney transplantation, the probability of biopsy-proven T-cell-mediated or antibody-mediated rejection within the next 12 months. It is meant to help transplant teams decide whom to review first, not to diagnose rejection; biopsy with Banff classification remains the reference standard, and imaging such as CT is not part of the input.
