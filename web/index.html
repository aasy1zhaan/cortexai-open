Clinical basis

Built on markers transplant centres already use

Default alert thresholds come from published work and every centre can tune them. The model is trained on a synthetic cohort today and needs prospective validation on real recipients before clinical use.

dd-cfDNA ≥ 1%

The fraction of donor-derived cell-free DNA most often used to separate normal from abnormal results, with high negative predictive value for rejection.

Kant & Brennan, Transplant International 2022
DSA MFI ≥ 1,400

The binary donor-specific antibody cut-off inside the iBox prognostication system, which combines eGFR, proteinuria, DSA and Banff lesions.

Loupy et al., BMJ 2019
Class II eplets ≥ 14

Living-donor recipients above this load developed de novo class II DSA far more often (20.2% vs 3.4%).

Sakamoto et al., Frontiers in Immunology 2020
Tacrolimus CV > 30%

High intrapatient variability and time below range are associated with rejection and graft loss; published cut-offs span roughly 23–40%.

Benning et al., Frontiers in Immunology 2026
